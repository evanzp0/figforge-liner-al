2.4 Permutations and Transposes
1 A permutation matrix P has the same rows as I (in any order). There are n ! different orders.
2 Then Px puts the components x1, x2, …, xn in that new order. And PT equals P−1.
3 Columns of A are rows of AT. The transposes of Ax and AB are xTAT and BTAT.
4 The idea behind AT is that Ax · y equals x · ATy because (Ax)Ty = xTATy = xT(ATy).
5 A symmetric matrix has ST = S. The product S = ATA is always symmetric.
